FAQ

Is the app in English?

Yes. The app and the phone app come in English and Korean, following your Windows (and phone) language. You can switch in Settings → Display → Language.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). macOS is not supported yet.

How many PCs can I use one key on?

One PC. When you change PCs, remove the key in Settings → About and register it on the new PC.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to register the license key and to download updates. Everything else works offline.

Windows shows an "unknown publisher" warning

The app is made by an independent developer and is not code-signed yet. Click "More info → Run anyway" to install.
