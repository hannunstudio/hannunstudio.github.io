Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-03

1. What the app stores

Hannun Studio stores your library list, viewing history and settings only on your PC. It does not send them to the developer or any other server, and it has no usage analytics or ad tracking.

2. When the app goes online (only when you turn it on)

  • License activation: your license key and device name are sent to Lemon Squeezy's license server.
  • Update check: the app asks the update address whether a new version exists (only the app version is revealed).
  • AI translation: if you add your own API key and enable it, subtitle text (not video or audio) is sent to the provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI). The key is stored encrypted on your PC.
  • Phone connection: your phone and PC talk directly on your network; nothing passes through the developer's servers.

3. Payment information

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. The developer never sees or stores your card details. To issue license keys and handle refunds, the developer receives your email address and order information.

4. Contact and deletion

Email yns747@gmail.com to ask about or delete your purchase records. Data the app keeps on your PC is removed by uninstalling it or deleting the %APPDATA%\HannunStudio folder.
